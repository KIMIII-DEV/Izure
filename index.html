FAQs

What services do you offer?
I provide end-to-end brand strategy, graphic
design, visual storytelling, copywriting, and custom
photo/video assets—all tailored to build cohesive,
impactful brand experiences.
How do you structure pricing for client work?
Every project is scoped individually — I share a fixed
quote after a short discovery call.
How long does a typical project take?
Most brand engagements run four to eight weeks,
depending on scope and review cycles.
Are you open to full-time, part-time, contract, or
remote roles?
Yes — I am open to all four, as long as the brand and
the brief are a good fit.
Are you open to relocating for a full-time role?
For the right role, yes. Let’s talk about the details.
